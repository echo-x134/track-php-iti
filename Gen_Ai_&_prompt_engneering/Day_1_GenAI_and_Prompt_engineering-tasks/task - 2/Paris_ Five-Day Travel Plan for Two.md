# Paris: Five-Day Travel Plan for Two

## Planning assumptions

The trip has five days and four nights, because the dates were not supplied. The €2,500 budget is treated as a total trip budget for two people, including a provisional €700 allowance for round-trip travel to Paris but excluding shopping. Hotel costs assume a mid-range double room in a central or well-connected neighborhood at approximately €140 per night. Exact transport and attraction prices can vary with date, booking channel, and eligibility; official rates are cited where verified.

The plan favors walking and public transportation, groups nearby sights, avoids more than one major ticketed museum on most days, and preserves a slower evening or meal period daily.

A café breakfast and a short hotel rest period should separate arrival logistics from sightseeing. The day is intentionally light because arrival times are unknown.

### Day 2 — Louvre, Tuileries, and Palais Royal

| Time | Plan | Duration | Transport | Estimated cost for two |
|---|---|---:|---|---:|
| Morning | Timed Louvre entry, concentrating on a planned route rather than attempting the whole museum. | 3 h | Metro or walk, depending on hotel | €44 ticket assumption |
| Afternoon | Lunch near Palais Royal; Tuileries Garden and Place de la Concorde; return to hotel for a rest. | 3 h including lunch | Walk | €30 meal allowance |
| Evening | Aperitif and dinner around the Marais or central Paris. | 2.5 h | Metro or walk | €50 meal allowance |

The Louvre is open 09:00–18:00 Monday, Thursday, Saturday, and Sunday; 09:00–21:00 Wednesday and Friday; and closed Tuesday. Last entry is one hour before closing, so the visit should be assigned to a date that is not Tuesday and booked in advance [1].

## Accommodation recommendation

The best balance for this budget is a mid-range hotel near République, Bastille, the Latin Quarter, or the 9th arrondissement. These areas offer good metro connectivity, a broad restaurant range, and lower prices than the immediate Louvre or Eiffel Tower area. A €140 nightly assumption for four nights produces an estimated €560; a €700 allowance is retained to absorb seasonal variation, city tax, and date-specific pricing.

## Transportation recommendation

For 2026, Île-de-France Mobilités lists a full-price Metro–Train–RER ticket at €2.55, a Paris Region–airport ticket at €14, and an all-zone Navigo Week pass at €32.40 [3]. For a five-day visit with an airport journey at each end, budget approximately €116.80 for two people: two weekly passes (€64.80) plus four airport-region tickets (€56). The exact cheapest combination depends on airport and whether the travel dates align with the weekly pass validity period. Walking should cover many central-area transfers.

## Food strategy

Use bakeries or hotel breakfast for €6–€10 per person, a sit-down lunch for approximately €15–€20 per person, and reserve one or two €25–€35 per-person dinners for classic bistro or regional French food. Suggested experiences include a croissant-and-coffee breakfast, crêpes in the Latin Quarter, a market picnic, cheese and pâtisserie tastings, and one traditional bistro meal. Restaurant names should be selected close to the final hotel after dates are known, because opening days and reservations change.

## Practical tips and risks

Book the Louvre and Eiffel Tower on official websites as soon as dates are known. Recheck closures and timed-entry requirements before each visit. Keep valuables secure in crowded metro stations and tourist areas, and use a zipped cross-body bag. Leave extra time for airport security and do not schedule a major attraction immediately before a flight. Verify whether Notre-Dame interior access, special exhibitions, or attraction hours have changed. Carry comfortable shoes, expect stairs and uneven paving, and reserve restaurants for peak evening times.
